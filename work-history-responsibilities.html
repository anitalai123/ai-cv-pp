If you are using AI to help you

Find the advert for the job you want, or one that is similar. Look for the key words that are sprinkled through it. Use those exact words when you describe your responsibilities.

If you only have a little experience that fits the job, it’s worth including. Mention it and emphasise the skills you used. That will make it more relevant.

Check the text and make sure what you use is accurate and truthful.

Remember an employer is looking to recruit you, so your CV works best for you if it’s authentic.
