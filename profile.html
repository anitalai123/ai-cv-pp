Write a personal profile

Write 2 or 3 short sentences that:

  • mention the job title, organisation or field you want to work in
  • outline your experience, personal qualities and transferable skills
  • show how your specific skills match the job description

For example:

An experienced bookkeeper and administrator. Enthusiastic team player, with a flexible attitude and project management experience. During career break took the opportunity to further develop computer skills and now seeking to resume full-time employment within an accounts environment.
