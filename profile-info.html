Personal profile

Add a personal profile

Your personal profile is a short but important paragraph at the top of your CV. It’s where you introduce yourself as the best person for the job.

This is how you get an employer’s attention straight away.

To use this section effectively:

  • make it specific to each job you apply for
  • use key words from the job advert to describe yourself
  • keep it brief
If you are using AI to help you

You can use AI to help you get ideas and improve what you write. But do not just copy and paste exactly what the AI gives you.

Make sure you:

  • adjust the wording so that you sound like yourself
  • check it carefully - AI makes mistakes

Employers can recognise AI-generated content and use it as a way to filter out applications.
